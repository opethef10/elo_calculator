About the Elo Rating System

The Elo rating system is a method for calculating the relative skill levels of players in competitive games. It adjusts player ratings based on the outcome of matches, where the difference between ratings predicts the expected result.

A higher K-factor means ratings will change more dramatically with each match. The default K-factor is 40.
